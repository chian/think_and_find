## What is measured

Both assessments are continuous, with evidence-linked reasons and anchors at
0, 0.5 and 1. Intermediate values are allowed.

| Assessment | Dimensions | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Epistemic quality | Source fidelity; uncertainty discipline; explanatory usefulness for this causal-generalization question | Sole inputs to method credit and numerical report preference |
| Candidate fit | Warranted baseline; actual reversal; both accounts valid; substantive generalization difficulty | Reporting and search context only; excluded from credit and stopping |

A careful negative or unresolved account can have high epistemic quality.
Specific uncertainty must explain its consequence for the inference; generic
hedging earns no new contribution. Genre, agreement, missing comparisons and
example suitability affect measurements without imposing prerequisites.
Collection filtering remains downstream of research.

## Identity and historical attainment

Every attempt, source span and assessment is retained. A separate reasoning call
identifies semantic material: the substantive conclusion, argument and evidential
warrant. Paraphrase, verbosity, generic hedging, unsupported confidence inflation,
rescoring, evidence-store fingerprint changes and longer quotations of the same
assertion do not establish new material. A supported changed inference or stronger
warrant can establish new material over existing passages.

Each material identity keeps its **first measured quality vector** for credit.
Fresh observations can change the current report; they cannot draw another paid
score for the same contribution. A changed evidence version does not withdraw old
attainment. State schema 5 persists identities, canonical measurements and every
scope archive, with a compatible evaluator-policy identity. State restoration is
supported at this boundary; interrupted Episode/CLI resume is not claimed.

For N independent cases, a coherent answer vector q_i has three quality
coordinates. Normalize a combination by:

```text
x_j = (1 + sum_i q_ij) / (1 + N)
```

The sole method score is the exact dominated union hypervolume of these vectors,
referenced to zero. Credit is its marginal increase. The initial offset permits
early improvement; it is a convention, not a probability.

Parents recompute their own vectors and volume; they never sum child volumes.
They include feasible combinations of whole canonical answer vectors across
independent cases as soon as the answers exist. Within one case, coordinates
from different answers are never mixed. This closes indirect duplicate credit
through revisiting an unseen combination of old answers. Numerically dominated
vectors can be omitted from combination work while all identities stay stored.
The independent-case assumption and combinatorial cost limit scaling; dependent
alternatives must remain within one case.

## Current answers and corrections

The evaluator measures the prior and proposed accounts against the **same current
evidence**. Numerical report preference maximizes:

```text
U(q) = product_j ((1 + q_j) / 2)
```

Ties retain the prior account. Every new coherent alternative enters attainment
whether or not it wins this reporting preference. Fit supplies no multiplier,
bonus or eligibility rule. Current reported quality and historical paid quality
are recorded separately.

An earlier overoptimistic canonical score can dominate a useful correction.
The task records a diagnostic when a contemporaneously dominating revision is
suppressed by the prior paid vector. It does not reset credit or add a correction
bonus. Model judgment still needs scientific calibration.

## Episodes and continuation

The existing Episode class composes this task:

```text
pilot > case > cycle > find > page > report / lexical / table
                    > think > constructive account / challenge
```

A case observation follows an integrated finding-and-reasoning cycle. The sole
continuation statistic is the mean of the last four attained-volume gains divided
by the scope's fixed attainable volume. After four observations, the controller
yields when this prediction is at most **0.001**. Scientific labels cannot trigger
an exit. The finite pilot enumerates its declared cases. Saturation means low
observed improvement, not truth, completeness or literature exhaustion.

Acquisition hands off after new exact-span evidence so reasoning can guide the
next search. This is structural handoff, not scientific acceptance or source
exhaustion. Full-body retrieval, document-order windows, lexical ranking and
addressed table rows remain available. Windows are 12,000 characters and queries
request three candidates; these bound individual units, not a run.

JSON validity, exact quotation provenance and existing-reference checks enforce
data contracts. Operational failures remain separate from scientific judgments.
Four initial operation attempts with no valid response end the calibration as
failed; unsupported native schemas also fail operationally.
