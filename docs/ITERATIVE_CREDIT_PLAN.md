## Binding requirements

- Preserve every attempt and evidence record while awarding no second credit
  for duplicate work, rediscovery, or restoration of an unchanged conclusion.
- Use continuous task quality and a single marginal hypervolume method credit.
  Scientific adequacy, source genre, agreement, missing comparisons, and example
  suitability must not gate acquisition, reasoning, or Episode closure.
- Continue according to numerically predicted improvement. Apply collection
  filters only to reporting after the improving search has ended.
- A more accurate conclusion, including a well-supported correction, can improve
  an answer even when its suitability as a positive example decreases.
- Keep source text, claims, reasoning, uncertainty, current answers, historical
  credit, and operational failures distinguishable.
- Do not create a test suite or validate by replaying old artifacts. Verify
  changes by static checks and registered live experiments with numerical rules.

## Phase 1: duplicate credit

Introduce durable result identity and historical coherent quality attainment.
Keep event persistence independent of marginal credit. Repeated judgments,
evidence-fingerprint changes, and returning to a previously credited quality
must add zero credit. Avoid independently maximizing incompatible coordinates.
Preserve current scientific state separately from historical attainment.

## Phase 2: continuous quality and continuation

Replace boolean scientific credit and acceptance/rejection closure with continuous
task-specific quality. Read unresolved comparisons and source quality into the
assessment rather than mandatory slots. Use one numerical improvement statistic
for continuation; document its window, threshold, and interpretation. Keep all
search modes, source coverage, and parent-child identity ownership intact.

## Phase 3: improvements through correction

Separate candidate suitability from the quality of the developing research answer.
Measure evidential accuracy, calibrated uncertainty, and explanatory usefulness.
Credit substantive supported revisions without paying for wording, confidence
inflation, or a repeated assessment. Preserve the final scientific limitations.

### Remaining implementation contracts

The point-2 planner located scientific gates in both `Source.next_case` and
`Controller.observe`, as well as the boolean projection itself. All three must
change. Four continuous candidate-fit measurements replace source-anchor,
agreement, and qualification bits. The four-observation prediction will use a
registered normalized marginal-volume tolerance of 0.001; continuous scores have
no smallest positive increment. Case observations must integrate acquired
evidence before counting an improvement observation. Historical status remains
reporting metadata only. These are task-local changes, not a new Episode engine.

The point-3 planner separates evidence fidelity, uncertainty discipline, and
explanatory usefulness from candidate fit. Both old and proposed interpretations
must be considered against the same current evidence. A semantic measurement
cache prevents another score draw for the same argument from becoming new
attainment. All proposals remain in the audit even when their credit is zero.

The archive remembers paid attainment; it does not certify historical judgments.
An earlier overoptimistic score can dominate a useful correction. The live
correction experiment must measure this possibility explicitly, rather than
concealing it with a correction bonus or independently maximized coordinates.
An explicitly labeled live-model overgeneralization stimulus on freshly fetched
evidence can test the correction boundary; it is not a scientific conclusion.
Paraphrase, generic hedging, verbosity, unsupported certainty, and a return to
an old answer must add zero credit. Positive correction credit requires a
better measured coherent answer, not the occurrence of a correction event.

Phase-2 verification will check that semantic states cannot close a case and
that numerical saturation exits satisfy the registered window and tolerance.
Before phase-3 data collection, the correction planner and implementer revised
the proposed fit-decrease test: the warrant-inclusive fit rubric can legitimately
increase when an inaccurate overclaim becomes an accurate negative account.
Forcing its decrease would test the wrong thing. Method credit will use the
three epistemic dimensions only, with explanatory usefulness explicitly tied
to the causal-generalization question. Fit remains reporting and query context.

Phase-3 verification will preregister a supported semantic correction, mean
same-evidence epistemic-quality increase >=0.15, positive marginal volume, and
zero volume for repeated/cosmetic/unsupported-confidence variants. Fit change
will be reported, never required to be positive or negative. Structural checks
will verify that fit cannot enter credit or stopping. These are experiment
success criteria; none is an eligibility threshold in the acquisition loop.
Every coherent proposed vector enters attainment independently of which answer
is numerically preferred for the current report. If an earlier overoptimistic
paid vector suppresses a correction, report that calibration failure without
re-awarding restored volume or adding a correction bonus.

The follow-up duplicate audit found a parent-only route: revisiting a known
answer after another case changes can form a previously unseen combination of
old answers. Its volume could increase although no case learned anything.
Phase 3 will register a coherent offered snapshot only when a material identity
is first recorded for that case. Other cases use their canonical first measured
vectors. Fact arrival, another rating, and report selection alone cannot register
a point. New proposals enter attainment even if the prior answer remains the
preferred report. Restoration must include every expected scope archive.

A second check found that first-registration-only offers are insufficient when
a genuinely new answer has an old or dominated quality vector. The stronger
parent rule is to include all feasible combinations of coherent per-case
canonical vectors as soon as those answers are available. Each independent case
contributes one entire answer vector; coordinates within a case are never mixed.
Numerically dominated per-case vectors can be pruned from the combination work
without removing their identity or audit history. This makes parent attainment
independent of report-selection order and cannot be implemented by summing child
volumes. It assumes the registered cases are independent contributions to the
collection; dependent alternatives belong within one case. The small calibration
scope permits this computation; combinatorial cost is a scaling limitation.

The correction experiment will include a second live case so old-material
returns can check both case and parent credit, and will serialize/restore before
later repeated assessments. All stimulus answers and variants will be generated
by the real configured model over newly retrieved evidence; a deliberate
overgeneralization is labeled as an experimental stimulus outside its answer.
No recorded provider output or synthetic response provider is used.
