## Assessment and improvement candidates

The formalism improves the record: source reports and inferences are separate,
both local frames have explicit dependencies, and the ant near miss was
recognized. It is not yet a reliable search engine for a demonstrated
reversal. A free-text bridge can be imagined after two different results are
found; lack of evidence for that bridge is then described as a translation
failure. The rival account can be marked `challenged` while its own text says
that ordinary additional variables explain the observations. Theory credit
can rise from a polished comparison of unresolved cases.

The next scientific changes should be evaluated against constructed positive
and near-miss arguments before deployment:

1. Make the transported inference an addressable, warranted argument step.
   Record the premise that makes the transfer plausible, the exact prediction
   it yields, and evidence for or against that prediction. A different
   intervention or an unlicensed extrapolation cannot by itself be the
   reversal witness.
2. Test a specified fixed-vocabulary rival against a discriminating
   observation. If that rival explains both observations while the transported
   prediction lacks warrant, classify the case as unresolved or reconciled;
   do not turn missing bridge evidence into defeat of the rival.
3. Let a search pursue the currently decisive proof obligation, whether it
   concerns an existing case or a new system. Log how the question, literal
   query, source choice, and next argument revision connect. Reward an
   evidence-backed resolution, including a consequential negative finding,
   within the coherent-material credit calculation. Do not reward extra
   steps or force a fixed research sequence.
4. Tie `source_kind` to the document read. A review's summary of a cited
   experiment remains review-level evidence until the original study is
   acquired. Seek independent source support for pivotal bridge and target
   inferences.
5. Compare case and theory gains with their weakest actual proof obligations.
   A cross-case theory built only from provisional cases should not receive
   strong nontransportability credit because its language is coherent.

The [exploration audit](EXPLORATION_AUDIT.md) states how to check these
changes in later logs without confusing query count, topic diversity, or
retrospective feedback with a scientifically warranted reversal.
